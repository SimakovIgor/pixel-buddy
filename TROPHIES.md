# Trophies

Fourteen trophies, eight open and six secret. Each one brings a gold cup and fireworks in its rarity color, and
`share` in the menu copies a card to show it off. The trophy case in the menu shows found ones in color, open ones
in gray and secret ones as a question mark until you find them.

Rarity is in the badge shape: a round badge is rare, a hexagon is epic, a shield is legendary.

Times are your local time. Clicks work the same in every client: in the terminal and the desktop app you click the
buddy or next to it, in VS Code and Cursor you use the buttons next to it.

## Open trophies

| Trophy | Rarity | How to earn it |
|---|---|---|
| 🦉 Night Owl | rare | commit between 2:00 and 5:00 at night |
| 🚀 Friday Deploy | rare | commit on a Friday after 18:00 |
| 📈 Comeback | rare | get green tests within 15 minutes after three failed runs in a row |
| ⚽ Ball Champ | rare | catch ten balls within one minute: click next to the buddy, or press ⚾ in VS Code. A ninja catches shurikens, they count too |
| 📅 Regular | epic | feed your buddy every day for a week: any snack counts, and every answer from Claude drops one |
| 🌳 All Grown Up | epic | get a buddy to level 10: answers, green tests and commits all give xp |
| ✨ Shiny! | legendary | have a shiny buddy in your nest. One egg in a hundred hatches shiny, new eggs come from the menu |
| 👑 Legend | legendary | have a legendary buddy in your nest. One egg in a hundred hatches legendary |
